shorts-uploader

shorts-uploader is a private, single-owner tool. Its owner uses it to upload short educational videos about U.S. prices and economic data (based on official government statistics) to the owner's own YouTube channel, and to read that channel's public statistics (views, likes, subscribers) to plan future videos.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
